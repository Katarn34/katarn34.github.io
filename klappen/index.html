Gameplay & Technical Design

I implemented the core gameplay systems and mechanics, starting with the building system, turning design intentions into robust, scalable technical solutions.

I also took part in Game Design decisions: prototyping features, iterating on mechanics and keeping every player interaction in line with the project’s vision and tone.

UI Programming & UX

I designed and built the interfaces with a focus on clarity, accessibility and player comfort: UI logic structure, integration with gameplay systems such as the Inventory Menu, and refinement through playtesting.

Throughout, I worked closely with the Designers and Artists, balancing technical constraints, design goals and player experience.

01Spawning from inventoryData Table → Preview Actor
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

One table drives every buildable piece

Selecting a piece in the inventory reads its row in a Data Table: mesh, construction data and cost. That data feeds a single, generic preview actor, so adding a new piece never needs new code.

02Line trace & snappingValidation
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

Snap first, validate always

A continuous line trace checks the surface under the cursor. Box colliders placed on each structure act as snap points, and the preview turns red as soon as a placement is invalid.

[GIF · snapping]
[Caption]
03Grid fallbackTransform
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

When there is nothing to snap to

If no snap point is found, a math function rounds the hit location to a world grid, so free-standing pieces still line up. The preview turns green when the placement is valid.

0490° rotationPlaytest-driven
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

Less freedom, more fun

Full 360° rotation was tested first, then limited to 90° yaw steps. Preset variants cover the other orientations.

05Placement pipelinePlayerController → Player
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

Two steps, two owners

The PlayerController checks the build mode and limits placements per input. The Player logic then spawns the final actor and plays its build animation. Input rules and world changes stay separate.

06HISM conversionOptimisation
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

Far away, structures become instances

Structures outside the player’s overlap sphere are converted into Hierarchical Instanced Static Meshes, cutting actor and draw-call overhead. Their data is kept in a storage system.

The player never sees the swap

When the player comes back, the stored instances are swapped back into full actors within a single frame, so nothing pops.

08Destruction & refundHold to remove
Draft — to be replaced with your Carrd text

Removing should feel as good as building

A timed hold removes a structure and refunds its resources. The hold gets shorter with each removal in a row, and a dynamic material highlights the target.
