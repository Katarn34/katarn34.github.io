My Role

Gameplay & Technical Design

I implemented the core gameplay systems and mechanics, starting with the building system, turning design intentions into robust, scalable technical solutions.

I also took part in Game Design decisions: prototyping features, iterating on mechanics and keeping every player interaction in line with the project’s vision and tone.

UI Programming & UX

I designed and built the interfaces with a focus on clarity, accessibility and player comfort: UI logic structure, integration with gameplay systems such as the Inventory Menu, and refinement through playtesting.

Throughout, I worked closely with the Designers and Artists, balancing technical constraints, design goals and player experience.

System 01 · 8 steps · post-mortem

Building System

From picking a piece in the inventory to removing it with a refund: snapping, a math grid fallback, a two-stage placement pipeline, and an HISM conversion that keeps large villages cheap.

  • 19 pieces, editable by Designers in a Data Table
  • Snapping points + virtual math grid
  • Actors → HISM beyond 2,000 units
  • 20+ Blueprint graphs, profiled in Unreal Insights

Then · Lessons learned

Lessons learned

Freedom is not creativity
Total building freedom left players lost. A snapping grid made building readable, at the cost of precision we should have handed back to the player.Read the post-mortem ▷
Optimise for the real need
Our HISM conversion worked, but the average session was too short to ever need it. Measure the real play session before optimising.Read the post-mortem ▷
